How Odds Are Calculated

This calculator uses a Monte Carlo simulation method:

  1. It runs 50,000 simulated poker hands
  2. For each simulation, it randomly deals the remaining unknown cards to complete the community cards and opponent hands
  3. It evaluates your best 5-card hand against all opponents' best hands
  4. It counts how many times you win, tie, or lose
  5. The final percentages are calculated from these results

The more simulations run, the more accurate the results. This method accounts for all possible card combinations that could appear.
